Mnemora Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

The short version: your decks, cards (including any pictures you add), study history and settings are saved only on your device. There's no account, and there are no ads, analytics, tracking or cookies. Mnemora never asks about your own health, and nothing you study is sent to us.

Who we are

Mnemora (the web app at mnemora.happyheartsoftware.com and the Mnemora Android app) is made by Happy Heart Software LLC, doing business as Happy Heart Software, based in Ohio, USA ("Happy Heart Software", "we", "us"). This policy covers Mnemora only.

What stays on your device

  • Your decks and cards, including cards you create or edit, tags, and your study notes.
  • Pictures you add to your own cards. Mnemora shrinks each picture and saves it on your device. Saving it this way also removes hidden photo details, such as where a photo was taken.
  • Your study history: when you reviewed each card, how you rated it, which hints you used, and when it's due next.
  • Your settings, such as motion preferences and study limits, and which version of the study disclaimer you accepted, and when.

This is kept in storage on your device (IndexedDB, in your browser or inside the Android app). We never receive it, so we can't see it, recover it or restore it for you. Mnemora also saves its own app files on your device so it works offline.

Backups

Settings, Your data, Export backup saves everything, including the pictures on your cards, as one file that you keep wherever you choose. Backup files aren't encrypted, so store them somewhere you trust. Restore reads a backup file on your device; the file isn't uploaded anywhere.

Health information

Mnemora teaches about medications as a study subject. It's for learning only and isn't medical advice. Mnemora never asks about your own health, conditions or medications, and it has no feature for tracking them. Please keep personal health details out of your study notes. If you add them anyway, they stay on your device. If you're in crisis in the US, call or text 988 to reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

Contacting us

The contact link in Mnemora opens our contact form at forms.happyheartsoftware.com, outside the app. If you send it, your email address, the topic you pick, your message and your name (optional) are delivered to our inbox through Heartforms, our own form service, which keeps a copy so we can answer you. The form page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser information when you open it. Please don't include personal health information. The Email us link opens your own email app instead.

Study reminders (Android app)

If you turn on a daily reminder in Settings, the Android app asks your phone for permission to show notifications. Your phone schedules and shows the reminder; nothing is sent over the internet. The reminder doesn't say what you're studying. Reminders are off until you turn them on, pause while vacation mode is on, and can be turned off in Settings or in your phone's settings at any time.

Updates to the app

When you open Mnemora, it checks mnemora.happyheartsoftware.com for a newer version and asks before updating. In the Android app, this check reads a small version file from the same website and, if there's a newer version, offers a download link. This sends nothing about you beyond the request itself.

What we don't do

There are no ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting, and Mnemora doesn't set cookies. The app contains no third-party code that contacts other companies: its fonts are part of the app, and it never loads pictures from the internet, even if a card links to one. Pictures you add to your own cards come from your device and stay there. We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.

If we ever add a feature that uses an outside service, such as searching a public research database, it will be off until you turn it on, and Mnemora will tell you exactly what would be sent, and to whom, before anything is sent.

Analytics

Mnemora doesn't use any analytics tool, whether our own or another company's. It doesn't count visits, record which screens or features you use, or give you an identifier, and it contains no tracking code. The only records of visits are the standard request logs our hosting provider keeps for security, described under Services involved below.

Services involved

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and keeps request logs, including IP addresses, for security. We don't use Cloudflare Web Analytics in Mnemora.
  • Heartforms, our own form service, receives contact form messages and keeps a copy until we delete it. It runs on Cloudflare and sends the email through Resend. Our inbox is hosted by Google Workspace.

Deleting your data

  • Delete any card or deck in the app.
  • Settings, Your data, Delete all my data permanently erases everything Mnemora stored on that device. We can't undo it.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, also removes everything Mnemora stored on that device.
  • To delete messages you sent us, email contact@happyheartsoftware.com.

Children

Mnemora isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. Mnemora doesn't collect personal information from anyone. Some study content discusses medications and mental health and is written for older teens and adults. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email contact@happyheartsoftware.com and we'll help. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, and we'll honor them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top. If a change affects how Mnemora handles your information, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Email contact@happyheartsoftware.com.
