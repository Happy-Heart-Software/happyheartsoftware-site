About us

Happy Heart Software is a small, independent software studio based in Ohio. We make simple, focused apps for the everyday parts of life: keeping track of money, getting your creative work out into the world, looking after your body and mind, and having a little fun.

We think good software should feel like a helpful friend, not another chore. So every app starts with one question: what's the next small step you actually need to take? Then we build just that, in plain language, and leave out the rest.

We also build custom software and websites for small businesses, creators, nonprofits and individuals. See how we can help
