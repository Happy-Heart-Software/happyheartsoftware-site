Our privacy promise

Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

The short version: this website doesn't use cookies or ads, and doesn't track you. We count visits with a privacy-friendly tool that doesn't identify you. The only personal information we collect is what you choose to send us through the contact form or by email, and we use it to reply to you. We never sell it.

Who we are

This website, happyheartsoftware.com, is run by Happy Heart Software LLC, doing business as Happy Heart Software, based in Ohio, USA ("Happy Heart Software", "we", "us"). This policy covers this website only. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy; see privacy for our apps.

What we collect

What you send us. When you use our contact form, we receive your name, email address, the topic you pick and your message. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include.

Visit counts. We count visits to this website without identifying you. See "Analytics" below for exactly what's recorded.

What we don't collect. This site doesn't set cookies, doesn't use advertising tools, and doesn't build a profile of you.

Basic technical information. Like any website, the services that deliver this site automatically handle technical details such as your IP address and browser type so the page can reach you and stay secure. See "Services we rely on" below.

Analytics

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It's run by Cloudflare, which also provides our domain name service, not by an advertising company. It doesn't use cookies or store anything on your device, doesn't give you an identifier, and doesn't follow you to other websites, so there's nothing that could link your separate visits back to you. It records, in total across all visitors: which pages were viewed, the referrer (the link that brought you here, if any), general browser, operating system and device type, approximate country (worked out from your IP address, which isn't used to identify you), and how quickly pages loaded. This tells us which pages people find useful, without identifying who visited them.

Only this website uses it. Our apps don't use Cloudflare Web Analytics or any other analytics tool, unless that app's own privacy policy says otherwise.

How we use your information

  • To read and reply to your message.
  • To follow up on a project or question you've asked us about.
  • To keep a record of our conversations with you.
  • To protect the site and our inbox from spam and abuse.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

Services we rely on

We use a few trusted services to run this site. Each handles information under its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and may keep server logs, including IP addresses, for security.
  • Cloudflare provides our domain name service and our visit counts (Cloudflare Web Analytics).
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on this site. Your browser requests the fonts from Google, which receives your IP address and browser information.
  • Heartforms, our own form service, receives what you submit through the contact form, keeps a copy so we can reply, and emails it to us. It runs on Cloudflare and sends email through Resend.
  • Google Workspace hosts our email, where your messages are stored.

When we might share information

Beyond the services above, we share personal information only if the law requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a sale or transfer of our business, in which case this policy would continue to apply.

How long we keep it

We keep messages for as long as we need them to respond, work with you and keep reasonable business records. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we have about you. Email contact@happyheartsoftware.com and we'll help. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we'll honor them.

Children

This website isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

This site is served over a secure (HTTPS) connection, and we limit who can access your messages. No method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, so please don't send passwords, payment details or other highly sensitive information through the contact form.

Links to other sites

If we link to another website, that site's own privacy practices apply, not this policy.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.

Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Email contact@happyheartsoftware.com.
